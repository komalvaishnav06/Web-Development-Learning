Prehistoric and Ancient India

Human activity in the Indian subcontinent dates back over two million years, with early stone tools found in the northwestern regions and Soan River valley, and evidence of Homo erectus in the Bhimbetka rock shelters more than 100,000 years ago Wikipedia Wikipedia . Sedentary life began around 7000 BCE, leading to the Indus Valley Civilization (2500-1900 BCE) in present-day Pakistan and northwestern India, known for urban planning, metallurgy, and trade Wikipedia Wikipedia +2 . Following its decline due to drought, Indo-Aryan tribes migrated into northern India around 1800-1500 BCE, initiating the Vedic period (1500-500 BCE), which saw the composition of the Vedas and the development of the varna system
